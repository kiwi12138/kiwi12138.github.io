<h2 style="margin: 60px 0px 10px;">Short Bio</h2>

I am a researcher at the Life Simulation Research Center, [Beijing Academy of Artificial Intelligence (BAAI)](https://www.baai.ac.cn/en/). I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the [University of Liverpool](https://www.liverpool.ac.uk/) in 2024, advised by Prof. [Xi Yang](https://scholar.xjtlu.edu.cn/en/persons/XiYang01/), Prof. [Kaizhu Huang](https://faculty.dukekunshan.edu.cn/faculty_profiles/kaizhu-huang), Prof. [Qiufeng Wang](https://scholar.xjtlu.edu.cn/en/persons/QiufengWang/), and Prof. [Frans Coenen](https://www.csc.liv.ac.uk/~frans/).

My research focuses on **reliable AI under distribution shifts**: how learning systems can adapt to non-stationary, unlabeled data streams and generalize to environments unseen during training. My recent work extends this focus to **multimodal large language models and agents**, studying their capabilities, failure modes, and evidence-grounded reasoning in real-world workflows.

In **medical imaging**, I develop methods and systems for generalizable image analysis, patient-specific **3D/4D cardiac reconstruction**, and quantitative assessment of anatomy, motion, function, and tissue. I led [CardioLens](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.00123), a hospital-scale benchmark for evaluating multimodal models across the cardiac MRI workflow, and contributed to [BAAI Cardiac Agent](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.04078) for automated CMR interpretation. I work closely with clinicians, imaging specialists, and industry partners to connect methodological advances with clinically grounded evaluation and applications.

## Research Interests

- **Test-Time Adaptation and Domain Generalization**
- **Reliable Multimodal Large Language Models and Agents**
- **Medical Image Analysis and Clinical Reasoning**
- **3D/4D Cardiac Reconstruction and Quantitative Analysis**
- **AI for Science**
